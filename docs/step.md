# UStep component contract / UStep 组件契约

> Status / 状态：Private pre-release declarative single-step projection.
> 状态：私有预发布声明式单步骤投影。

`UStep` presents caller title, description, index, and finite visual `status`. With explicit `interactive` and readable title it reports a local `select` intent; it can coexist with `USteps` without parent injection.

`UStep` 呈现调用方 title、description、index 与有限视觉 `status`。显式 `interactive` 且 title 可读时，它报告本地 `select` 意图；可与 `USteps` 共存且不使用父级注入。

It owns no workflow, automatic advancement, submission, navigation, or business completion conclusion. The root namespace is `u-step` and consumes `--u-comp-step-*`.

它不拥有工作流、自动推进、提交、导航或业务完成结论。根命名空间为 `u-step`，消费 `--u-comp-step-*`。
